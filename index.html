Under the Hood

How It's Built

Sites are blocked at the DNS level and verification runs entirely on your device — nothing you do gets sent to a cloud service.

7 Categories: Social, Gaming, Gambling, Video, Shopping, News, and Memes — block any combination in one click, plus a permanent baseline you can't turn off.

Whitelist: Keep specific tools working even when their category is blocked (LinkedIn, Chess.com, Discord).

Verified Server-Side: Unlocks are signed and checked on the server, so the client can't just fake a pass.
